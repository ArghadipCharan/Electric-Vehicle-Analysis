# ⚡ AtliQ Motors — Electric Vehicle Market Analysis in India

**Code Basics Resume Challenge #12**

A detailed market study to help **AtliQ Motors**, a leading US-based automotive giant, launch its bestselling EV models in India by analyzing the current EV/Hybrid market landscape.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The project provides deep insights into India’s electric vehicle (EV) market, covering penetration rates, sales trends, growth rates, revenue forecasts, seasonal demand, and strategic recommendations for expansion.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Identify states with positive & negative penetration rates in EV sales (2022–2024).
- Analyze quarterly sales trends for major EV makers.
- Calculate Compounded Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) for top makers & states.
- Estimate revenue growth rates for EVs in India.
- Determine peak and low seasons for EV sales.
- Provide strategic recommendations for AtliQ Motors’ market entry.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Overview

The dataset covers:
- **Timeframe:** 2022–2024
- **Vehicle types:** 2-wheelers & 4-wheelers
- **Metrics:** Sales volume, penetration rates, growth rates, quarterly trends
- **Geography:** All Indian states with detailed breakdowns

---

## ❓ Research Questions

**Primary:**
1. Top 3 and bottom 3 makers (2023 & 2024) by 2-wheeler sales.
2. Top 5 states with highest penetration rates in 2- and 4-wheeler EV sales (FY 2024).
3. States with declining (negative) EV penetration (2022–2024).
4. Quarterly sales trends for top 5 4-wheeler EV makers.
5. Compare EV sales & penetration rates in Delhi vs. Karnataka (2024).
6. CAGR for top 5 4-wheeler makers (2022–2024).
7. Top 10 states with highest CAGR for total vehicles sold (2022–2024).
8. Peak & low sales months for EVs (2022–2024).
9. Projected 2030 EV sales for top 10 states by penetration rate.
10. Estimated revenue growth rates for 2-wheelers & 4-wheelers (2022 vs. 2024, 2023 vs. 2024).

**Secondary:**
1. Key reasons for choosing 4-wheelers in 2023 & 2024.
2. Impact of government incentives & subsidies.
   - States providing highest subsidies.
3. Correlation between charging infrastructure & EV sales in top states.
4. Ideal brand ambassador for AtliQ Motors in India.
5. Best state for setting up a manufacturing unit.
6. Top 3 recommendations for AtliQ Motors.

---

## 📈 Insights & Findings

- Clear identification of states with strong EV penetration growth and states experiencing decline.
- Quarterly sales analysis shows distinct trends across top makers.
- CAGR highlights top-performing makers and states leading the EV adoption curve.
- Seasonal sales peaks and low-demand months are mapped for supply chain planning.
- Revenue forecasts for 2022–2024 provide clear financial growth expectations.
- Analysis of factors influencing customer choices: environmental impact, oil independence, noise & air pollution reduction.
- States offering the highest subsidies are mapped for strategic planning.
- Virat Kohli identified as the ideal brand ambassador due to high brand value and alignment with sustainability messaging.
- Recommendations include location suggestions for manufacturing based on ease of doing business, governance, and subsidies.

---

## ✅ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** for data analysis and Visualization
- **MS PowerPoint** for presentation and reporting

---

## ✅ Recommendations

- **Brand Positioning:** Partner with a high-value, eco-conscious ambassador (Virat Kohli) to appeal to India’s younger audience.
- **Market Entry:** Focus on states with high growth rates and positive penetration.
- **Infrastructure:** Invest in charging stations to boost sales in top states.
- **Manufacturing Hub:** Choose states with favorable subsidies, stable governance, and ease of doing business.

---

## 📚 Results

This project provides clear, actionable insights for **AtliQ Motors** to:

- Identify high-opportunity states for EV launches.
- Understand market trends, seasonal patterns, and customer behavior.
- Align production, supply chain, and marketing to growth pockets.
- Make data-driven strategic decisions for a successful India expansion.
- Strengthen brand positioning through sustainability messaging and local partnerships.
